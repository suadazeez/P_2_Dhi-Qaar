# Forbidden_Patterns.md

## ALCHEMY Forbidden Academic Patterns

Remove or reconstruct sentences containing:

- It is important to note that
- It should be noted that
- In this paper, we propose a novel
- This paper aims to fill that gap
- plays a crucial role
- a wide range of
- a growing body of literature
- significantly enhances
- immense potential
- is paramount
- demonstrated strong performance
- shows strong performance
- What this means in practice:
- What this means in plain terms:
- So we treat
- So the answer is
- This paper presented
- confirming the effectiveness of
- highlighting the importance of
- making it suitable for
- Furthermore / Moreover / Additionally as repeated sentence openers

## Structural Patterns to Avoid (not just wording)

These are AI-detector-flagged even when no forbidden phrase appears, because the *shape* of the sentence is predictable regardless of author:

- Parallel section-roadmap sentences ("reviews X (§2), formalizes Y (§3), describes Z (§4)...") — replace with roadmap prose that varies verb form and connects sections by what depends on what, not a flat list.
- Literature-paragraph openers that start with a general historical/definitional claim before any citation — lead with the specific claim a citation supports instead.
- Generic closing-sentence transitions ("These approaches trade different resources... and are complementary to...") — state the actual trade-off directly, not the category of trade-off.
- Table/figure captions that all follow the identical template sentence-for-sentence — vary caption syntax the same way body prose is varied.
- Any sentence whose only job is to summarize what a subsection is about to do or just did, with no new claim inside it.

## Replacement Strategy

Do not mechanically replace only words.
Rewrite the sentence architecture.
Preserve claims, numbers, and citations.
