## Purpose

A writing framework for producing academic prose with the clarity, specificity, and scope-awareness of a careful researcher. The objective is genuine writing quality. Reduced false-positive AI-detection flags are a possible by-product of good writing, **never the objective**, and this framework must not be used to misrepresent authorship or to defeat institutional integrity checks.

## Principles

1. Every claim earns its place.
2. Numbers must be interpreted, not decorated.
3. Sentences vary naturally.
4. Paragraphs should not end with generic summaries.
5. Scope and limits must be explicit.
6. Evidence strength controls claim strength.
7. Structural predictability is a defect independent of wording — vary roadmap sentences, citation-cascade openers, and captions the same way sentence architecture is varied elsewhere.

## Required Texture

Each substantial paragraph should include:
- one short sentence
- one longer analytical sentence
- at least one specific consequence or boundary
- no repeated mechanical opening pattern

## Protected Content

Never alter:
- numbers
- citations
- equations
- dataset names
- architecture names
- acronyms
- metric values
